List 100

A running list of a hundred things I want to do, see, learn, and try — inspired by Chip Huyen and, honestly, a lot of movies. Some are enormous. Some take an afternoon. A few of them I only understood the point of afterwards.

The number is aspirational, not a deadline. I keep adding to it, and every so often I get to draw a line through something.
