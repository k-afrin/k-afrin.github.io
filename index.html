I grew up in a small mining town in Jharkhand, India, dreaming about doing big things and seeing the world. I've been chasing that dream ever since!

Research & applied work

Machine Learning Projects

Most of these began as questions from my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering at Texas A&M — how do you learn something trustworthy from data that is rare, imbalanced, incomplete, or collected from a body in motion? A few of them grew into patents and products.

assets/projects/
random-survival-forest.jpg
PROJECT 01

Building a Random Survival Forest for Extremely Unbalanced, Right-Censored Data

Rare-event survival data breaks most off-the-shelf models: the events you care about are a tiny minority, and many observations are censored before anything happens at all. This work develops a balanced random survival forest approach for exactly that regime.

Survival analysis Imbalanced data Right censoring Ensemble methods
assets/projects/
system-wide-rsf.jpg
PROJECT 02

Manufacturing System-wide Balanced Random Survival Forest

Extending balanced survival modelling from a single machine to an entire manufacturing system — so that reliability and failure prediction account for how components fail together, not just in isolation.

Reliability Prognostics Manufacturing systems

PROJECT 03

Prognosis of Epileptic Seizure

Predicting seizures early enough to matter, from wearable signal. This line of work became a wearable AI headband that forecasts epileptic seizures and alerts clinicians — with a provisional patent issued.

Patented Wearables Time-series AI Clinical ML

PROJECT 04

SpO₂ Estimation Using Neck Photoplethysmography

Estimating blood-oxygen saturation from PPG signal captured at the neck rather than the fingertip — the sensing foundation behind a patented AI wearable for sleep apnea detection.

Patented Signal processing PPG Sleep apnea
