About

I'm an AI Product Manager with a Ph.D. in Industrial & Systems Engineering, specializing in AI/ML. Over the past six-plus years I've turned technical and AI-powered solutions into business impact — from leading McKinsey's Gen AI Central Development Hub and Data & AI transformations at acting Engagement Manager scope, to taking an AI product from concept to FDA Breakthrough Device designation at Ceribell as a Senior Product Manager.
