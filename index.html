Hi, I'm Afrin! 👋

AI Product Manager·Consultant·Ph.D.·Reader·Writer·Traveler·Dreamer

I currently work as an AI Product Manager at Ceribell, building AI products for stroke detection. Before that, I was a consultant in McKinsey's Digital & Analytics practice. And before that, I was a Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at Texas A&M, where I focused on AI and machine learning.

Somehow, through this fairly interdisciplinary, and not exactly planned, path, I've ended up working at the intersection of technology, product, and business strategy. It turns out I like it here.

I love trying new things and collecting new experiences. Recently, that has included turning my love for the written word into PoAIm, a tiny AI poem-generating app, and going to candlelight concerts for the first time (highly recommend).

I grew up in a small mining town in Jharkhand, India, dreaming about doing big things and seeing the world. I've been chasing that dream ever since!

Research and applied work

Machine Learning Projects

Most of these began as questions from my Ph.D. How do you learn something trustworthy from data that is rare, imbalanced, incomplete, or collected from a body in motion? A few of them grew into patents and products.

assets/projects/
random-survival-forest.jpg
PROJECT 01

Building a Random Survival Forest for Extremely Unbalanced, Right-Censored Data

Rare-event survival data breaks most off-the-shelf models. The events you care about are a tiny minority, and many observations are censored before anything happens at all. This work develops a balanced random survival forest approach for exactly that regime.
